Fig. 04 · API

One call. Ten cameras.

Free, no key, CORS open. Send a GPS point, get the closest public cameras back, or get redirected straight to the nearest feed.

GET/api/nearest?lat=&lon=

The 10 closest cameras, nearest first, with distance and direction. Optional: limit (1–200), radius_km, category (traffic, scenic, water, weather, other), source (any part of a source name, e.g. tfl, caltrans), media (image or video).


    
GET/api/go?lat=&lon=

      
Redirects (302) to the closest camera's feed. Add &video=1 to land on the live stream or clip, or &image=1 for the still image. Accepts the same filters as /nearest.

    
GET/api/go/{id}

      
Redirects to one camera by id. Every result from /nearest includes its go link.

    
GET/api/cameras?id=

      
One camera's full record: feed, still image, HLS stream, MP4 clip or embeddable player URLs. Without an id, returns dataset totals.
